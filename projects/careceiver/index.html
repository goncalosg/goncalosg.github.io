Careceiver

Project type UX/UI Design
My contribution Wireframing | Website design
Tools Figma | Notion
Year 2024

Careceiver is a startup focused on supporting informal caregivers. They partnered with my college to collaborate with students on a rebranding project and the design of a digital ecosystem. Due to the project’s scale, the class was divided into different departments. I was part of the UX/UI Design team, where I contributed to research, wireframing, interface design, and usability testing.

Challenge

Reimagining a User-Centered
Care Platform

The careceiver project focused on redesigning an existing website that lacked clarity, consistency, and intuitive navigation. The original platform presented usability issues across both desktop and mobile, making it difficult for users to complete key tasks efficiently. Our challenge was to rethink the structure and user flow, simplify interactions, and improve accessibility while maintaining a modern and cohesive visual identity. Working as a team of five, we aimed to transform a fragmented experience into a clear, responsive, and user-centered platform grounded in strong UX principles.

Approach

Building a Strong UX Foundation
Through Research

In this phase, user-centered research was conducted to better understand usability issues and user needs. Through interviews and analysis, we identified key pain points that informed the restructuring of the website’s information architecture and navigation. Based on these insights, we developed a clear website map and created low-fidelity wireframes to explore and validate layout solutions for desktop and mobile. Multiple iterations were made before moving into high-fidelity prototypes, ensuring a cohesive, responsive, and user-focused final design.
